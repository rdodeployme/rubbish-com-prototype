How it works

Like getting quotes for any other job, without ringing around.

  1. Post what needs to go

    Add your items or a few photos, your suburb and when suits. It's free and takes about two minutes.

  2. Collectors send quotes

    Verified collectors near you see the job and send you a price. Your address stays private until you book.

  3. Pick one and it's gone

    Compare price, reviews and timing, then book. You pay after the job's done.

Why book with us

Every collector is checked

We verify each collector's identity, driver's licence, vehicle registration and insurance before they can quote.

$1,500 dumping guarantee

If a collector booked through us dumps your rubbish illegally, we cover the costs and fines you incur, up to $1,500.

97% kept out of landfill

Collectors are pointed to the nearest place to recycle or reuse each material, instead of straight to the tip.

Questions

What is Rubbish.com.au?

A marketplace for rubbish removal. You post what needs to go, and verified local collectors send you quotes. You choose who does the job.

What does it cost?

Posting a job is free. You pay the price in the quote you accept, after the job is done.

When can I book?

We're signing up collectors in Melbourne and Brisbane first. Post your job now to join the waitlist, and we'll let you know as soon as you can get quotes.

Will collectors see my address?

Collectors see your suburb so they can quote. Your address is only shared with the collector you book.

What can't be collected?

Asbestos, chemicals, paint, gas bottles and other hazardous waste. Rules can vary between states and territories.
