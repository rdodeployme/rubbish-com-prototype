What is Rubbish.com.au?

A marketplace for rubbish removal. You post what needs to go, verified local collectors send quotes, and you choose who does the job.

What does it cost?

Posting is free. You pay the price in the quote you accept, after the job is done.

When can I book?

We're signing up collectors first. Post your job now to join the waitlist and we'll let you know when you can get quotes.

What can't be collected?

Asbestos, chemicals, paint, gas bottles and other hazardous waste. Rules can vary between states and territories.
