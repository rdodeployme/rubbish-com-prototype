AN EXAMPLE JOB
  1. 1 · Post

    Post what needs to go.

    List the items or add photos, your suburb and when suits. It's free and takes about two minutes.

  2. 2 · Quote

    Local collectors send prices.

    Verified collectors nearby quote on the job. Your address stays private until you book.

  3. 3 · Book

    Pick the one that suits.

    Compare price, reviews and timing. Cheapest isn't the only option. Choose what works for you.

  4. 4 · Collect

    They turn up and do the lifting.

    Your collector arrives at the booked time and loads everything. You don't need to carry a thing to the kerb.

  5. 5 · Gone

    It's gone. Then they're paid.

    The collector is paid once the job's done, and they're pointed to the nearest place to recycle or reuse what they took.
