How it works

Post job for free. Choose best quote. Pay after pickup.

1

Post a job for free

Snap a photo to create a listing. Add what's there, your suburb, a date and a time window: 5am – 8am, 8am – 12pm, 1pm – 3pm or 3pm – 5pm. Collectors send quotes in seconds. Your suburb is shown so that collectors can locate you — no address is shared until you accept a quote.

2

Choose best quote

Compare prices and reviews of different collectors. Tap any collector to see their star rating and the jobs they've completed, then choose the best.

3

Pay after removal

Message each other securely. Pay digitally after your junk removal — not before.

4

Green disposal

Waste is reused or recycled. Your collector gets the nearest drop-off for each material, and full documentation is provided.

1

Register

Sign up with your ABN, waste carrier licence, vehicle and registration, area and contact details. We verify your identity, waste carrier licence and insurance before you can quote.

2

Browse jobs and quote

See every open job near you on the jobs page. Filter by distance, price, size, time window and material, then send your price.

3

Win the job

When a customer books you, you get a job pack: the address, time window, what's there, and the nearest drop-off for each material — cardboard, polystyrene, mattresses, green waste, e-waste, metal and general waste.

4

Do the job, build your rating

Collect in the booked window and dispose at licensed facilities. Every completed job and star rating shows on your profile, so good work wins more work.

What we can't take

Every customer confirms their job has none of these before posting:

  • Asbestos
  • Explosives and ammunition
  • Poisons and chemicals
  • Gas bottles and fuel
  • Paint and solvents
  • Medical and clinical waste

Questions

How much does it cost to post a job?

Nothing. You only pay the price you accept from a collector.

What if I only get one or two quotes?

You can book any quote you've received, or wait for more. We aim for 3 but it depends on how many collectors cover your area that day.

How are collectors checked?

We verify every collector's identity, waste carrier licence and insurance details before they can quote, and their star rating builds from real completed jobs.

Do I have to share my phone number?

No. You can message collectors on the platform without disclosing personal details.

Who can see my address?

Your suburb is shown so that collectors can locate you. No address is shared until the job poster accepts the quote.

What if my waste gets dumped illegally?

If waste is illegally disposed of by a collector via this platform, we will cover any costs and fines you incur, up to $1,500 AUD.

Where does my rubbish go?

Collectors get the nearest drop-off for each type of material with every job, so recyclable items can go to the right place instead of straight to landfill.
