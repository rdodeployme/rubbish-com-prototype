How it works

Post your rubbish, get quotes from local collectors, book one. That's it.

1

Post your job

Tell us what needs to go, roughly how much, what it's made of, and your suburb. Add a photo if you can — it gets you more accurate quotes. Posting is free.

2

Pick a day and time window

Choose the date and one of four windows: 5am – 8am, 8am – 12pm, 1pm – 3pm or 3pm – 5pm.

3

Get quotes

We aim to get you 3 quotes from registered local collectors. Sometimes you'll get more, sometimes fewer. Each quote shows the collector's price, star rating and completed jobs.

4

Check the collector, then book

Tap any collector to see their profile, reviews and past jobs. Book the one you want — you don't have to wait for all 3 quotes.

5

It's gone. Rate them.

Your collector turns up in your window and takes it away. Afterwards you rate the job out of 5 stars, which helps the next customer choose.

1

Register

Sign up with your ABN, area, vehicle and contact details. We check your ABN and public liability insurance before you can quote.

2

Browse jobs and quote

See every open job near you on the jobs page. Filter by distance, price, size, time window and material, then send your price.

3

Win the job

When a customer books you, you get a job pack: the address, time window, what's there, and the nearest drop-off for each material — cardboard, polystyrene, mattresses, green waste, e-waste, metal and general waste.

4

Do the job, build your rating

Collect in the booked window and dispose at licensed facilities. Every completed job and star rating shows on your profile, so good work wins more work.

What we can't take

Every customer confirms their job has none of these before posting:

  • Asbestos
  • Explosives and ammunition
  • Poisons and chemicals
  • Gas bottles and fuel
  • Paint and solvents
  • Medical and clinical waste

Questions

How much does it cost to post a job?

Nothing. You only pay the price you accept from a collector.

What if I only get one or two quotes?

You can book any quote you've received, or wait for more. We aim for 3 but it depends on how many collectors cover your area that day.

How are collectors checked?

We check each collector's ABN and public liability insurance before they can quote, and their star rating builds from real completed jobs.

Where does my rubbish go?

Collectors get the nearest drop-off for each type of material with every job, so recyclable items can go to the right place instead of straight to landfill.
