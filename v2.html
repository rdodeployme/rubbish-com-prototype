How it works

Post job for free. Choose best quote. Pay after pickup.

  1. Post a job for free

    Add a photo, what's there, your suburb, a date and a time window: 5am – 8am, 8am – 12pm, 1pm – 3pm or 3pm – 5pm. Collectors send quotes in seconds. Your suburb is shown so collectors can locate you. No address is shared until you accept a quote.

  2. Choose the best quote

    Compare prices and reviews from different collectors. Tap any collector to see their star rating and the jobs they've completed. Choose the one that suits you best.

  3. Pay after removal

    Message each other securely. Pay digitally after the job is done, not before.

  4. Responsible recycling

    Where possible, waste is recycled, reused or redistributed. Your collector gets the nearest drop-off for each material.

  1. Register

    Sign up with your ABN, waste carrier licence, vehicle and registration, area and contact details. We verify your identity, driver's licence, vehicle registration and insurance details before you can quote.

  2. Browse jobs and quote

    See every open job near you. Filter by distance, size, time window and material, then send your price.

  3. Win the job

    When a customer books you, you get a job pack: the address, time window, what's there, and the nearest drop-off for each material.

  4. Do the job, build your rating

    Collect in the booked window and dispose at licensed facilities. Every completed job and rating shows on your profile, so good work wins more work.

Questions

Is posting a job free?

Yes. Posting a job and getting quotes is free. You only pay the collector you choose, after the job is done.

Do collectors see my address?

Your suburb is shown so collectors can locate you for quoting. No address is shared until you accept a quote and engage the collector.

How many quotes will I get?

We aim for 3 quotes from verified local collectors on every job.

When do I pay?

After the job. You pay digitally once your rubbish has been removed, not before.

Where does my rubbish go?

Collectors get the nearest drop-off for each type of material with every job, so recyclable items can go to the right place instead of straight to landfill.

How are collectors checked?

We verify every collector's identity, driver's licence, vehicle registration and insurance details before they can quote. Collectors are rated by customers and by Rubbish.com.au based on their recycling performance.

What if my rubbish is dumped illegally?

If waste is illegally disposed of by a collector via this platform, we will cover any costs and fines you incur, up to $1,500 AUD.
