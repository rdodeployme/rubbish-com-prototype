How a job works

  1. Post

    The customer lists what needs to go, their suburb and when suits.

  2. Quote

    Verified collectors nearby send a price. The address stays private.

  3. Book

    The customer compares price, reviews and timing, and books one.

  4. Gone

    The collector takes it away and is paid once the job's done.
